1....Reverse a string.
answer :

rev="";

    rev=i+rev;

3....Count vowels and consonants.
answer : 

n1=input("Enter the word : ");
countvowel=0;
countconsonents=0;
for i in n1:
    if(i=="a" or i=="e" or i=="o" or i=="u" or i=="i"):
        countvowel+=1;
    else:
        countconsonents+=1;
print("The vowel : ",countvowel);
print("The consonents : ",countconsonents);


4....Character frequency.
answer :
