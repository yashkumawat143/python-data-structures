Medium Level dictionary....



1....Loop through a dictionary and print keys only.
answer : 

n1=input("Enter the name : ");
n2=int(input("Enter the age : "));

student={
    "name":n1,
    "age":n2
}
for key in student:
    print(key);

12....Reverse the key-value pairs of a dictionary.
answer :

n1=input("Enter the name: ");
n2=input("Enter the city name : ");

student={
    "name":n1,
    "city":n2
}
reverse={}
for keys,values in student.items():
    reverse[values]=keys;
print(reverse);
