13....Check whether tuple is palindrome or not.
answer : 

n1=input("Enter the element : ");
answer=tuple(n1.split());
for i in answer:
    ans=i[::-1];
    if(i==ans):
        print("it is a palindrome number =",i);
    else:
        print("it is not a palindrome number = ",i);

19....Flatten a nested tuple.
answer :

n3=input("Enter the third elements : ");
answer1=tuple(n1.split());
answer2=tuple(n2.split());
answer3=tuple(n3.split());

num=();
for i in t:
    for j in i:
       num+=(j,);
print(num);
