5....Separate even and odd numbers.
answer :

answer=n1.split();
even=[];

for i in answer:
    if(int(i)%2==0):
        even.append(i);

        odd.append(i);

1....Reverse list without using reverse()
answer : 

n1=input("Enter the elements : ");
answer=n1.split();
rev=[];
for i in answer:
    rev=[i]+rev;
print(rev);


2....Remove duplicates without using set()
answer : 

n1=input("Enter the elements : ");
answer=n1.split();
num=[];
for i in answer:
    if(i not in num):
        num.append(i);
print(num);
