5....Reverse the key-value pairs of a dictionary.
answer :

n1=input("Enter the name : ");
n2=input("Enter the city name : ");

student={
    "name":n1,
    "city":n2
}
rev={};
for key,value in student.items():
    rev[value]=key;
print(rev);

9....Create a dictionary using dictionary comprehension (squares of numbers 1–10).
answer :

    student[i]=i*i;
print(student);
